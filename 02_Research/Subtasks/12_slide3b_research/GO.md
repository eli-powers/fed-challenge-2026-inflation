## Locked decisions (Eli, Nov 14; binding)
- **Measure and horizon:** core PCE only, through 2027Q4 (8 quarters).
- **Baseline:** the **Q3 SPF** quarterly core PCE path.
  - The **Q4 SPF comes out Mon Nov 16**. If it is out before the run ends, rerun on it and show both.
  - Either way, the pipeline must switch to Q4 with **one rerun**.
  - The Q3 SPF predates the late-October oil spike, so the slide states the baseline's date.
- **Method:** outside forecast, adjusted by us and labelled. Two axes: **oil path × pass-through regime**.
  - Regimes: the oil note's **post-pandemic** vs **pre-pandemic** estimates.
  - Oil paths: (1) baseline; (2) oil rises again (e.g. back to ≥$110, per Bowman; quote her); (3) Hormuz reopens: pre-war trend plus a residual premium, anchored on Waller's $20–30 (quote him).
- **Adjustment:**
  1. Start from the baseline path.
  2. Measure each scenario's oil gap against the oil price the baseline assumed. Proxy: Brent futures as of the forecast date.
  3. Map the gap to core using the oil note's dynamics. Post-pandemic, a permanent 10% oil rise lifts the core price level about 0.35% after 12 quarters; pre-pandemic, about 0.05%. Use the note's coefficients quarter by quarter if its tables allow.
  4. Optional: map to headline through energy's PCE share.
- **Caveats to state:**
  - the oil note is a five-economy panel and an upper bound;
  - the baseline's own oil assumption is unpublished, so we use a proxy;
  - these are scenarios, not probabilities.
