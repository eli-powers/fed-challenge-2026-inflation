## Q1 Portfolio management method change
- BEA SCB, Mataloni, "The 2026 Annual Update of the National Economic Accounts", Sep 30, 2026 [O]: https://apps.bea.gov/scb/issues/2026/09-september/0926-nea-annual-update.htm (DOI 10.66137/BLNY5808). Saved: BEA_SCB_2026-09_annual_update_results_retrieved_2026-11-11.html
  - "BEA improved the deflation methodology for consumer spending on portfolio management and investment advice services to better reflect the timing and quantity of services consumed. The quantity of portfolio management services will be derived using a BLS Current Employment Statistics (CES)-based quantity extrapolator from the portfolio management and investment advice industry."
  - "The previous methodology used revenue data for portfolio management and investment advice services from Census to derive current-dollar estimates for consumer spending. A BLS producer price index (PPI) for portfolio management and investment advice was used for deflation."
  - "The improved method estimates the quantity of services directly by extrapolation using BLS CES employment and hours. Current-dollar estimates will continue to be derived using Census data, and the price index will be derived implicitly."
  - "For periods when Census revenues are not available, current-dollar estimates will be informed by market indicators and judgmental trend."
- Effective/revised range: release date Sep 30, 2026. SCB preview (Jun 24, 2026) [O] https://apps.bea.gov/scb/issues/2026/06-june/0626-nea-preview.htm: "each account will reflect the same update period: the first quarter of 2021 through the first quarter of 2026." BEA PIO August 2026 release (Sep 30) [O] https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-august-2026: "Today's release presents monthly results from the annual update of the National Economic Accounts. The revisions for estimates of personal income and outlays begin with January 2021."
  - BEA does not state, in the documents I opened, a month-by-month application of the new portfolio method to Apr-Aug 2026. What the release states: monthly revisions begin Jan 2021 (through Aug 2026, the first month published on the new methods).
- Corroboration from data (my own check, script B_vintage_revision_check.py, ALFRED vintages already in Raw): core PCE index levels for every month Nov 2025-Jul 2026 differ between the 2026-09-29 and 2026-09-30 vintages. July 2026 core m/m 0.246% -> 0.126%. This is the whole-vintage revision (all annual-update changes, not portfolio alone).
- Table 2.4.4U-D row 268 (E1PMI1): Feb 20, 2026 version "PPI Portfolio Management and Investment advice | NSA"; Sep 30, 2026 version "Implicit: Quantity extrapolation based on CES employment and hours data. Current-dollar extrapolation based on quarterly QSS data and judgmental trend. "Out months informed by market indicators and judgmental trend." | SA by BEA" [O] (3 files, see Q3).
- Secondary [2]: RBC (not relied on) says "will be applied retroactively through Q1 2021"; consistent with BEA.
- Other BEA docs: blog Aug 17, 2026 [O] https://www.bea.gov/news/blog/2026-08-17/annual-update-gdp-industry-and-state-stats-publicly-available-starting-sept-30 (same wording as preview).
- IMPLICATION FOR BACKTEST (fact only): first-print PCE for Dec 2025-Jul 2026 (published Feb 20 to Aug 26, 2026) was produced under the PPI method; Aug 2026 (Sep 30) and Sep 2026 (Oct 29) first prints under the new method. The Sep 2026 print states no method change.

## Q2 Legal services
- BEA FAQ 1486 "Does BEA adjust source data..." [O] https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/1486 (page last modified 4/9/26). Verbatim: "In recent years the CPI for legal services has been increasingly volatile and is no longer published by BLS. However, BLS continues to provide BEA with the unpublished CPI. In January 2026 the unpublished CPI showed an unprecedented increase that could not be substantiated by any other data. As a result, BEA made a downward adjustment to the PCE price index using changes in the BLS producer price index (PPI) for legal services to inform the adjustment."
- BEA PIO technical notes (saved pages, [O]):
  - Jan 2026 release page (https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-january-2026): no legal-services text in technical notes (my keyword scan).
  - Feb 2026 release: "An adjustment to the PCE price index for legal services was maintained for the month of January. No adjustment was made for the month of February."
  - Mar 2026 release: "The PCE price index for legal services was adjusted for the months of January and March. No adjustment was made for February."
  - Apr 2026: "adjusted for the months of January and March. No adjustment was made for February or April."
  - May 2026: "... No adjustment was made for February, April, or May."
  - Jun, Jul 2026: no legal-services statement in technical notes (keyword scan). Aug (Sep 30) and Sep (Oct 29) notes: none.
  - Release dates (from "Next release" chain and page stamps; page-last-modified stamps on Jan-Jun pages are all 8/14/26, i.e. not release dates): Oct+Nov 2025 data Jan 22, 2026; Dec 2025 data Feb 20, 2026 (page stamp 2/20/26); Jan 2026 data ~Mar 2026 (date not confirmed); Feb data Apr 9; Mar data Apr 30; Apr data May 28; May data Jun 25; Jun data Jul 30; Jul data Aug 26; Aug data Sep 30; Sep data Oct 29. The Feb-data release (Apr 9) is when the Jan adjustment is first stated in a technical note I opened. Release date for Jan data: not confirmed from page (page says nothing); Jan page lists "Next release: April 9, 2026".
- Important: the Employ America characterization ("legal services PPI data for January, and January alone" [2]) is NOT what BEA says. BEA says an adjustment (CPI-based index adjusted using PPI changes) was applied for Jan and March 2026, not Feb/Apr/May. Table 2.4.4U-D (Feb 20, 2026) still lists "CPI Legal services".
- Permanent change (Sep 30, 2026): SCB results article [O]: "BEA replaced the consumer price index (CPI) for legal services with an improved composite price index based on PPIs for legal services. This CPI was mostly unpublished since 2023 and was last published for the month of September 2024. BEA had been using unpublished CPI values provided by BLS, although these values did not meet BLS publication quality guidelines and have recently exhibited erratic changes that cannot be corroborated and are not consistent with other source data." PPIs: Civil negligence legal services; "Other legal services"; Wills, estate planning, and probate; Real estate legal services; Labor and employment legal services. "The weights used to combine these PPIs were based on unpublished detailed personal consumption expenditures (PCE) items within BEA's benchmark Input-Output Accounts."
- Preview (Jun 24) [O] says composite "will replace the consumer price index (CPI) for legal services beginning in 2024."
- Professional association dues (E1AXS1) follow the legal-services deflator: "Consumer spending on professional association dues is deflated using the PCE price index for legal services and therefore will also be impacted by this improvement." (preview, results article)
- BLS primary on CPI legal services: no BLS notice found (searched bls.gov via WebSearch [S]; CPI Handbook appendix snippet lists SEGD01 Legal services publication level; BLS Monthly Labor Review snippet says 26 percent basic cells missing Jan 2020-Jul 2023 for legal services). Not opened. "not found (unconfirmed)".
- Companion change (same table): Computer software and accessories deflator: "BEA-composite price index derived using the CPI for computer software and accessories; the PPI for game software publishing; and the PPI for hosting, ASP... " (table row 50: SA by BEA, was CPI NSA).
- Oct 2025 CPI imputation (relevant to the backtest window only because Oct 2025 CPI is missing): BEA PIO Oct+Nov 2025 release [O] (https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-october-and-november-2025): "To replace the missing CPIs, BEA derived seasonally adjusted price indexes for October using the geometric mean of the September and November CPIs." and "BEA derived non-seasonally adjusted price indexes by applying seasonal adjustment factors from October 2024 to the imputed seasonally adjusted values for October 2025."
